
SAMPLE VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES

This Agreement is entered into between ABC Company ("Business")
and XYZ Technologies ("Vendor").


2. SERVICES

The Vendor shall provide cloud-based customer management
services to the Business.

3. PERSONAL INFORMATION

The Business may provide the Vendor with customer names,
email addresses, telephone numbers and account information
for purposes of providing the contracted services.

4. USE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

The Vendor may use personal information to perform the
services described in this Agreement.

5. DATA RETENTION

The Vendor may retain customer information for as long
as reasonably necessary for its business operations.

6. INFORMATION SECURITY

The Vendor shall maintain reasonable administrative,
technical and physical safeguards to protect customer
information.

7. SUBCONTRACTORS

The Vendor may engage subcontractors to assist with
providing the services.

8. TERMINATION

Upon termination, the parties shall discuss the handling
of customer information.
